Onboard merchants to Bitcoin with managed LNbits accounts and automated payback arrangements!

Orange Piller helps Bitcoin evangelists set up merchants with their own LNbits wallets while automatically managing debt repayment through payment splitting.

Usage:

- Enable the Extension: Start by enabling the Orange Piller extension on your LNbits instance.
- Create an Arrangement: Set up a new merchant with a managed wallet, specifying the payback amount and percentage split.
- Automatic Repayment: Incoming payments to the merchant wallet are automatically split, routing the configured percentage back to the orange piller.
- Track Progress: Both the orange piller and merchant can view arrangement status and repayment progress on their respective dashboards.
- Automatic Completion: When the debt is fully repaid, rerouting stops and the arrangement is marked as completed.

Note:
The payback percentage determines what fraction of each incoming payment goes toward repaying the debt. The merchant keeps the remainder. Once the total repaid amount reaches the arrangement total, the merchant's wallet operates fully independently.
